GPU expert cache: MoE models have many experts, only subset activated per token. Host-resident experts can be uploaded to GPU cache slots. Eviction policy chooses which experts to keep in GPU cache. LRU evicts least recently used. LFU with decay per epochs: frequency counters decay over epochs/time, so experts used often in recent epochs remain, old hot experts fade. It balances popularity and recency. Pure LRU only recency.

Difference: LRU assumes locality of reference: recently used likely soon. LFU assumes frequency: frequently used likely soon. Decay makes LFU adaptive: not stuck with historically hot experts; epoch decay reduces stale counts, approximates recency-weighted frequency. For GPU expert cache, experts may have temporal patterns: phase changes, prompt-specific routing, long-term hot experts, bursty usage.

Workload where LFU-decay wins: MoE with stable hot experts over many tokens/epochs but interleaved with cold experts. LRU can be thrashed by cold experts: each cold expert becomes most recent and evicts hot expert; hot expert must be reloaded repeatedly. LFU-decay keeps hot experts because their accumulated frequency outweighs single recent cold accesses; decay prevents old experts from dominating forever. Mechanism: counters increment on hits; epoch decay multiplies counts or subtracts, so hot experts maintain high score; cold experts have low score despite recency.
