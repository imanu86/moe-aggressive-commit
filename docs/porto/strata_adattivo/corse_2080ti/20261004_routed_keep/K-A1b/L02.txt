GPU expert cache: MoE models have many experts, only subset activated per token. Host-resident experts stored in CPU/disk, GPU cache slots hold hot experts. Eviction policy chooses which experts to keep/upload. LRU: evict least recently used; assumes temporal locality, recent experts likely reused soon. LFU: evict least frequently used; assumes popularity, hot experts remain. LFU with epoch decay: counters decay over epochs to adapt to changing popularity, avoid stale hot experts, approximate recency+frequency.

Need difference: LRU only recency, LFU frequency with decay. For expert cache, expert activation patterns depend on token distribution, routing, layer, sequence. Some experts are globally hot (common topics), some hot only in phase. LRU can thrash if many experts used once in a burst; LFU can keep popular experts but may fail when phase changes. Epoch decay: counters reset/decay each epoch (e.g., time window, tokens, requests) so old frequency loses weight; combines popularity with adaptation.

Workload where LFU wins: stable skewed expert popularity, repeated queries over same domain, long decode with same hot experts, or many requests with same routing distribution. Example: server serving many prompts about code, certain experts activated frequently across requests; LRU may evict hot experts due to occasional cold experts or interleaved requests, causing repeated uploads. LFU keeps hot experts, lower miss/upload. Mechanism: frequency accumulates, hot experts survive; decay prevents unbounded bias but still favors high hit count.

Workload where LFU loses: phase-shifting or bursty locality, where experts used recently in current phase but not historically frequent; or adversarial/one-off cold experts. Example: sequential domain switch: first many prompts about legal, then many about medical; or a single long document with unique experts. LRU wins because recent phase experts are kept; LFU with slow decay may keep old legal experts and evict new medical experts, causing thrash. If decay too slow, stale popularity dominates.
