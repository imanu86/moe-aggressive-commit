GPU expert cache: MoE layers have many experts, only subset cached on GPU; host-resident experts uploaded on demand. Eviction policy chooses which cached experts to replace when new expert needed. Cost: upload from host to GPU expensive; want minimize misses/uploads.

LFU decayed: each epoch (e.g. token/step/window) counts hits; counters decayed so old hits lose weight. Evict lowest score. It can keep experts that are consistently popular across epochs, even if not used in last epoch. LRU keeps experts used most recently, regardless of overall popularity.
