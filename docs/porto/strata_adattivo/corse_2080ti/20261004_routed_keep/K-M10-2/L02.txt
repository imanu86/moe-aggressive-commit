LFU decayed: maintain access frequency per expert, periodically decay (e.g., multiply by alpha or reset per epoch). Captures popularity over window, not just recency. Workload where LFU wins: MoE routing with Zipf-like stable hot experts, many layers, long decode, repeated hot experts accessed across epochs, plus occasional cold experts (e.g., rare tokens, long context, diverse queries) that touch once. LRU: cold expert recently used evicts hot expert; then hot expert must be reuploaded repeatedly. LFU: hot expert frequency high, survives decay; cold low frequency evicted.
