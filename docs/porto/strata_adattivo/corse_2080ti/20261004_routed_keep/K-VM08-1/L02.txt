LFU decay: frequency approximates popularity over recent epochs; decay prevents stale popularity. LRU: keeps recently used; good for temporal locality but vulnerable to scan/one-off bursts and misses hot experts if evicted by recent noise.

Workload where LFU wins: stable hot experts with occasional cold experts/scan bursts. Example: MoE model with domain-specific routing: a few experts frequently activated for a prompt type, plus many experts touched once by rare tokens or long context. LRU evicts hot experts when cold experts are touched; LFU keeps hot because high counts, decayed but still above cold. Mechanism: hot experts accumulate counts over epochs; cold one-off counts decay; cache slots reserved for high frequency.

Workload where LFU loses: phase-shifting or recency-dominant workload: expert popularity changes over time, e.g. conversation topic switches, or streaming different domains, or each token uses different experts with no stable hot set but strong locality (recently used experts likely reused soon). LRU adapts quickly; LFU with decay may keep old hot experts too long, causing misses. Mechanism: decay not fast enough; old frequencies remain high, new experts need many accesses to enter; cache polluted by stale hot experts.
