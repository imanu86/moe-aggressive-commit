GPU expert cache: MoE models have many experts, only subset activated per token. Host-resident experts can be uploaded to GPU cache slots. Eviction policy decides which cached experts to keep. LRU: evict least recently used. LFU with epoch decay: frequency counters decay over epochs/time, evict lowest decayed frequency. It balances popularity and recency.

Difference: LRU locality of reference: recently used experts likely reused soon. LFU: frequently used experts likely reused often; decay prevents stale hot experts from occupying cache forever, adapts to phase changes. Epoch decay: counters multiplied or reset per epoch; old hits lose weight. It approximates sliding-window frequency.
